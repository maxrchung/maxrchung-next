Heroes is a simple reference site for Heroes of the Storm heroes, abilities, and
talents. I made this kind of because I got fed up with the ad-ridden, really
slow wiki sites. So I made my own little reference site stripped down to just
basic stuff that I cared about.

- Made by: Max Chung
- Made with: TypeScript, SolidJS, StyleX
- Repository: [https://github.com/maxrchung/heroes](https://github.com/maxrchung/heroes)
